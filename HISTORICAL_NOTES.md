# Historical notes — Badr scenario V2

This file records the research direction behind the expanded scenario. It is not a claim that every detail is equally certain. Early Sira and hadith reports differ in wording, chains, chronology and some numerical details. The in-game dialogue is explicitly written as a reconstruction, not as a verbatim quotation.

## Main points represented

- The expedition was initially connected to the Quraysh caravan returning from Syria; Abu Sufyan changed its route and sent word to Mecca. The Meccan force continued toward Badr after the caravan was safe.
- Traditional estimates for the Muslim force vary (often around 313, with other figures also transmitted); the Quraysh force is commonly described as around one thousand after withdrawals.
- The consultation with the companions is an important part of the pre-battle account. Reports include al-Miqdad's words and Sa'd ibn Mu'adh speaking for the Ansar.
- Reports describe reconnaissance near the wells and al-Hubab ibn al-Mundhir's advice about taking a position by the water, managing the other wells and preparing a basin.
- Rain on the night before the battle is referred to in Qur'an 8:11.
- The opening duel is reported with Utba, Shayba and al-Walid facing Hamza, Ali and Ubayda ibn al-Harith. Reports differ on the exact pairings; Ubayda was wounded and later died on the return journey in traditional accounts.
- Reports describe the general fighting, the attack on Abu Jahl by Mu'adh ibn Amr ibn al-Jamuh and Mu'awwidh ibn Afra, and Abdullah ibn Mas'ud's later encounter with him.
- Traditional accounts commonly report around seventy Quraysh killed, around seventy captured and fourteen Muslims killed. These are transmitted figures, not modern verified counts.
- The treatment of captives was discussed after the battle. Qur'an 8:67–68 addresses the question of captives.

## Gameplay abstractions

The playable map compresses the geography and troop numbers. Reserve waves are drawn from the Meccan force already represented on the field; they are a pacing device, not a claim that a new army arrived during the battle. The healing/cohesion effect is also a gameplay representation, not a depiction of unseen events.

## Uhud (chapitre 3)
Sources traditionnelles (Ibn Ishaq/Ibn Hisham, al-Bukhari) : sortie de Medine, retrait d'Abdullah ibn Ubayy avec ~300 hommes, ~50 archers sous Abdullah ibn Jubayr sur la colline, defi de Talha ibn Abi Talha, Hamza tue par Wahshi, depart des archers, charge de Khalid, rumeur de la mort du Prophete, Ubayy ibn Khalaf, appel d'Abu Sufyan, Hamra al-Asad le lendemain. Les chiffres sont des estimations ; les dialogues sont des reconstitutions. La branche "archers tiennent la colline" est un scenario alternatif signale comme tel dans le jeu.
