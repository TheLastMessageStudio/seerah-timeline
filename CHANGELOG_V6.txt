THE LAST MESSAGE — VERSION 6 (TACTICAL REWORK)
================================================

FOCUS OF THIS BUILD
-------------------
This is the first major gameplay pass of the Rebirth update. It prioritizes
battle depth and the expanded Badr scenario before the wider campaign, mode,
art and audio work.

1. BADR V1 PRESERVED
   - The original Badr chapter keeps its original victory condition and story.
   - The expanded Badr V2 rules are isolated to the separate badr_v2 chapter.

2. BADR V2 IS NOW A MULTI-OBJECTIVE CAMPAIGN
   - Capturing the well is not enough: hold it uncontested through two full
     player turn endings. Losing or having it contested resets the progress.
   - Reach the rain/night phase.
   - Defeat all three Quraysh champions: Utba, Shayba and al-Walid.
   - Survive both scripted reserve waves and eliminate every unit released
     by those waves. The reserve waves are explicitly a gameplay abstraction
     representing forces already on the battlefield, not a claim of historical
     reinforcements arriving after the battle began.
   - Defeat Abu Jahl. His defeat alone no longer ends Badr V2.
   - The objectives panel displays well-holding and reserve-unit progress.
   - Premature elimination of the visible enemy force cannot bypass the staged
     reserve waves in Badr V2.

3. ENEMY TACTICS
   - Quraysh's AI receives additional positioning incentives to contest the
     strategic well and keep infantry around Abu Jahl as a protective screen.
   - The existing AI continues to prioritize high-value targets, terrain,
     flanking opportunities and the protection of fragile support units.

4. TESTS ADDED
   - Badr V2 cannot be won by killing Abu Jahl alone.
   - The well objective requires two uncontested player turn endings.

BUILD STATUS
------------
- Source and ZIP structure can be checked in this environment.
- Flutter/Dart SDK is not installed here, so `flutter test`, Android APK
  compilation and Windows compilation have NOT been run. Please verify on the
  target machine before treating this as a release build.
