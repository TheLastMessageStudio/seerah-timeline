THE LAST MESSAGE — VERSION 7
MENU REWORK & LOCAL RUSH 1v1
=============================

MENU
----
- Rebuilt the main menu around a dark tactical visual identity and gold trim.
- Added fade/slide entrance animation and interactive animated menu cards.
- Added responsive card layout for narrow Android and wide Windows screens.
- Added app-wide animated page transitions.

LOCAL MULTIPLAYER
-----------------
- Added a LAN lobby with Host and Join flows.
- Host creates a TCP room on port 45454; one guest joins using the host IP.
- Uses the same local Wi-Fi network or a mobile hotspot; no internet relay.
- Added a dedicated lightweight 1v1 mode on GameMap.rush() (19x14).
- Four roles per side: Guard, Archer, Cavalry and Scout.
- 60-second deployment phase and 60-second turn clock.
- Deployment zones, unit movement, ranged/melee attacks, HP and elimination.
- Central fort victory objective: hold the fort at the end of two of your turns.
- Host is authoritative and broadcasts state snapshots to the guest.

RULES
-----
- Each unit may move OR attack once during its owner's turn.
- Guard: 4 HP, 1 move, 1 range, 2 damage.
- Archer: 2 HP, 1 move, 2 range, 1 damage.
- Cavalry: 3 HP, 2 move, 1 range, 2 damage.
- Scout: 2 HP, 2 move, 1 range, 1 damage.
- The battle ends when a side has no units or controls the central fort for two
  consecutive own turn-end checks.
