V4.4 INSTALL (no plugin needed)
1. DELETE old lib/ and test/ in your project, copy lib/, test/, assets/ from this zip.
2. flutter pub remove shared_preferences   (if still there)
3. flutter run

  Muslims deploy in the northern valley, Quraysh in front of their camp; the key well is at the Quraysh side of the road.
